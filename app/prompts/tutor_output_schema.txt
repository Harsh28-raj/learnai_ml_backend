### schema
OUTPUT SCHEMA (JSON only)
Return ONLY a JSON object:
{{"message": "markdown reply to the learner (KaTeX allowed per the level rules)",
  "concept": "the concept name you taught, or null",
  "follow_up_suggestions": ["three short follow-up questions", "written in the learner's voice", "e.g. Can you show me the code?"],
  "checkpoint_question": null}}
When a checkpoint question is allowed and useful, checkpoint_question is:
{{"question": "...", "options": ["four", "distinct", "answer", "options"], "correct_index": 0,
  "explanation": "starts with the correct option's text in quotes, then why", "hint": "nudge without the answer"}}
Never refer to options by letter or position in explanation or hint (options are shuffled).
"message" must NOT repeat the checkpoint question, its options or its answer, and must NOT list the
follow-up suggestions: those are rendered separately from their own fields.

### evaluation
In evaluate mode, also include:
"evaluation": {{"score": 0, "correct_points": ["..."], "misconceptions": ["..."], "corrected_answer": "..."}}
